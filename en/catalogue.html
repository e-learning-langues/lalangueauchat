How this site was made

The activities, experiments, observations and analyses presented here are our own: they come from our courses and our practice. Generative AI helped us rephrase and harmonise some of the texts; it also suggested a few additional ideas, which we kept or discarded, and it created the cat silhouettes. The site was built with Claude from our posters, then proofread and approved by the team. This English version was translated from the French with Claude; the French version remains the reference.
