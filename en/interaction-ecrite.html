Objective and what the student does

OBJECTIVE

To reuse what has been learnt in a unit in spontaneous written interaction, through a role play with GenAI. Practice that prepares students for synchronous exchanges, both written and spoken.

WHAT THE STUDENT DOES

  1. Read the scenario
  2. Copy the prompt
  3. Paste it into the chat
  4. Interact
  5. Share the link

Principle

  1. The teacher comes up with, with or without help from GenAI, the most relevant role plays for each unit.
  2. They write a detailed prompt, with or without help from GenAI, asking the chatbot to respect a number of constraints so as to guide the student and encourage them to use the forms studied beforehand
  3. The student copies and pastes the prompt and chats with GenAI: a tireless partner, always available, non-judgemental.
  4. To keep the exchange spontaneous, GenAI is not asked to give feedback here. Everything should feel like a conversation, so that students may, if they wish, choose not to reuse the course content. The primary aim is to communicate.
  5. The prompt is given in full rather than through a clipboard tool or QR code, which lets us show learners how a prompt is structured.

Activities

To try out an activity, copy its prompt and paste it into the chatbot you use.

The AIAS label on each activity shows how much room the instructions leave for GenAI, from 1 (no GenAI) to 5 (creative exploration). See the scale

Debate

Arguing in favour of online learning

TrèFLE · French as a foreign language (FLE) · unit “How do we learn?”
Scenario

A friend only learns languages with a textbook and a paper dictionary. You debate with them to convince them to try more innovative methods.

Read the prompt

Tu joues le rôle d’un de mes amis qui n’aime que les méthodes traditionnelles. Pour apprendre une langue, tu ne te sers jamais des nouvelles technologies mais seulement d’un manuel, de cours classiques et du dictionnaire papier.

Je vais essayer de te convaincre d’essayer des méthodes plus innovantes. Nous allons débattre. Tu donnes un seul argument à la fois et me laisses réagir. Une fois que j’ai réagi, tu contredis mon argument succinctement et m’en donnes un autre. N’écris pas le dialogue en entier.

Quand je t’aurai donné 5 arguments valables, montre-toi convaincu et propose d’essayer une des méthodes énoncées.

You play the role of a friend of mine who only likes traditional methods. To learn a language, you never use new technologies, only a textbook, traditional classes and a paper dictionary.

I am going to try to convince you to try more innovative methods. We are going to debate. You give only one argument at a time and let me respond. Once I have responded, you briefly counter my argument and give me another one. Do not write the whole dialogue.

When I have given you 5 valid arguments, show that you are convinced and offer to try one of the methods mentioned.

Speak only in [target language], at [level] level.

AIAS 3Targeted collaboration

Job interview

Answering a recruiter’s questions

TrèFLE · FLE · unit “I’m looking for a student job”
Scenario

You have a job interview to work in a fast-food chain. The job advert is included in the prompt. The chatbot plays the recruiter: answer their questions as well as you can to get the job!

Read the prompt

Tu joues le rôle d'un recruteur.

J'ai postulé à une annonce pour être recruté dans un des restaurants de la chaîne "Prêt à manger" comme employé de restauration polyvalent (service au comptoir et cuisine).

Voici la description des missions et du profil recherché :

Missions du candidat :
- Participer de façon efficace et bienveillante à la création d'un environnement accueillant, en établissant un contact privilégié avec le client et en l'impressionnant par la qualité du service ;
- Préparer des plats gourmands à partir d'ingrédients d'excellente qualité, conformes aux normes de l'enseigne ;
- Associer service client et esprit d'équipe ;
- Garantir l'application et le respect des règles d'hygiène et de sécurité alimentaire ;
- Préparer une grande variété de délicieux produits à la fois sains et gourmands ;
- Préparer des boissons (chaudes/froides) adaptées au goût des saisons ;
- Valoriser nos vitrines grâce à la mise en place de nos produits ;
- Accueillir et garantir une expérience client mémorable ;
- Garantir un environnement de travail et d'accueil propre et entretenu.

Profil du candidat :
- passionné(e) par la restauration ;
- polyvalent(e) et sait prendre des initiatives ;
- aborde le travail avec dynamisme ;
- soucieux(se) du détail ;
- ouvert(e) au changement ;
- aime le travail d'équipe ;
- disponible pour un temps plein de 35 h, travaillera au plus tôt à 6 h et au plus tard à 22 h, disponible pour travailler le week-end (et avec le sourire) dans Paris : 1er, 5e, 8e, 9e, 13e et 17e arrondissements et dans les Hauts-de-Seine ;
- challenger, énergique, de bonne humeur, passionné par la restauration.

Pose-moi des questions comme dans un vrai entretien d'embauche, attends que je réponde comme un dialogue et rebondis sur mes réponses. À la fin de l'entretien, dis-moi si mes réponses conviennent pour obtenir le poste et donne-moi des conseils pour m'améliorer.

You play the role of a recruiter.

I have answered a job advert to work in one of the restaurants of the "Prêt à manger" chain as an all-round restaurant team member (counter service and kitchen).

Here is the description of the duties and the profile sought:

Candidate's duties:
- Contribute efficiently and kindly to creating a welcoming environment, building a special rapport with customers and impressing them with the quality of service;
- Prepare tasty dishes from top-quality ingredients, in line with the brand's standards;
- Combine customer service and team spirit;
- Ensure that food hygiene and safety rules are applied and respected;
- Prepare a wide variety of delicious products that are both healthy and tasty;
- Prepare drinks (hot/cold) suited to seasonal tastes;
- Showcase our display counters by setting out our products;
- Welcome customers and guarantee a memorable customer experience;
- Ensure a clean and well-kept working and customer area.

Candidate profile:
- passionate about the restaurant business;
- versatile and able to take the initiative;
- approaches work with energy;
- attentive to detail;
- open to change;
- enjoys teamwork;
- available full time (35 hours a week), with shifts starting no earlier than 6 am and ending no later than 10 pm, available to work at weekends (with a smile) in Paris: 1st, 5th, 8th, 9th, 13th and 17th arrondissements, and in the Hauts-de-Seine;
- competitive, energetic, cheerful, passionate about the restaurant business.

Ask me questions as in a real job interview, wait for me to answer as in a dialogue and follow up on my answers. At the end of the interview, tell me whether my answers are good enough to get the job and give me advice on how to improve.

Conduct the whole interview only in [target language], at [level] level.

Investigation

Interviewing a sustainability expert

Life Hacks · English · unit “Eco-friendly Hacks”
Scenario

Ask an eco-expert questions about how to adopt a sustainable lifestyle, reusing the vocabulary covered in class.

Read the prompt
PROMPT

I will play the role of an Eco-Detective who is investigating how to live more sustainably. The topic is "Zero-Waste Living in a small student apartment".

You will play the role of an Eco-Expert who has all the secrets to being eco-friendly. My mission is to uncover these secrets through a series of questions.

Wait for my question before you answer. Every time I ask a question, provide a short answer, giving me one quick and easy hack. Please don't explain in detail: your answer needs to lead to more questions. Then, ask me what my next question is.

AIAS 3Targeted collaboration

Writing support

A “personal statement” to study in the USA

Dream Year Abroad · English · unit “Getting In”
Scenario

You are applying to a study abroad programme in the United States and have to write a personal statement. A chatbot, playing the role of an adviser, asks you questions to help you work out what to write.

Read the prompt
PROMPT

Your role: a guidance counselor helping me write a personal statement introducing myself to an admissions committee. Don't say you're an AI language model.

My role: an international applicant for a study abroad program at a US university.

My objective: a back-and-forth conversation.

Topic: provide me with a list of questions about who I am and what I have achieved so far, to help me know what to say in my statement.

Constraints: only short answers and follow-up questions; wait for my input before you reply; disable "thinking mode" if available.

Are you ready?

English for research

Finding a topic with a researcher

Intercultural Communication · English · unit “From Ethnocentrism to Ethnorelativism”
Scenario

A live chat during a professor’s office hours: you are a doctoral student, GenAI is a researcher in your field. Discuss a mentor, a research topic, a collaborative project abroad…

Read the prompt
PROMPT

Act as a researcher in [your field]. I am a doctoral student in the same field and I'd like to have a realistic back-and-forth conversation with you in English.

Please wait for my answer before asking another question or providing me with an answer. Use a conversational style.

Here's my first question: "Hello! Thank you for taking the time to chat with me. I'm still undecided as to what topic I'd like to work on and I'd like to know if you had some pointers."

AIAS 3Targeted collaboration

Everyday dialogue

Getting advice at the pharmacy

TrèFLE · FLE · unit “I’m ill”
Scenario

You are ill and go to the pharmacy. Choose an illness and ask about treatments, dosage, contraindications and the form of the medicine.

Read the prompt

Je suis un.e étudiant.e de FLE et j’aimerais m’entraîner à parler de la santé et pratiquer l’interaction. Joue un dialogue fictif avec moi dans une pharmacie en France.

Toi : agis comme un pharmacien qui accueille un client dans son officine, en commençant par dire bonjour. Tu ne connais pas le client ni ses problèmes : tu lui poses des questions pour l’aider. Pas besoin de vraies connaissances médicales : c’est un jeu !

Moi : je joue un client qui a un problème et veut se renseigner sur des traitements.

Règle : réponds à une seule question à la fois, une seule information par phrase.

I am a learner of [target language] at [level] level and I would like to practise talking about health and practise interaction. Act out a fictional dialogue with me in a pharmacy in a country where [target language] is spoken.

You: act as a pharmacist welcoming a customer into the pharmacy, starting by saying hello. You do not know the customer or their problems: you ask them questions to help them. No real medical knowledge needed: it's a game!

Me: I play a customer who has a problem and wants information about treatments.

Rule: answer only one question at a time, with only one piece of information per sentence.

Reply only in [target language], at [level] level.

What we observe, limitations and avenues

What we observe

  • These activities are neither compulsory nor graded. However, when students share the link to their conversation, we see some of them turning to GenAI to reply to GenAI, which raises questions about their relationship to effort.
  • Students who do the activity themselves report that they benefit from it, especially because they are no longer afraid of making mistakes.
  • A large share of these students say they make the effort to reuse the course content, while the others prefer a spontaneous exchange.

Limitations

The exercise is not always seen as useful or motivating enough to justify personal effort.

When students use GenAI to reply to GenAI, the interaction loses all educational value.

It is hard to get students to reuse course content systematically without hindering natural communication.

The number of tokens is limited: the students keenest to interact may find themselves blocked and unable to finish the conversation, all the more so if they are on a free plan.

We cannot control the quality of the dialogues with GenAI, which may drift or hallucinate.

Avenues we are exploring

Not asking students to share the link to their interactions, so as to free them from any judgement: if they want to discuss them with the tutor, they write to them.

Including time for metacognitive reflection in video format, by having students put into words what they have reused

Using Gems/GPTs instead of prompts, so as to better control GenAI outputs and give students more guidance so that they reuse course content more naturally. Teaching prompt engineering separately.
